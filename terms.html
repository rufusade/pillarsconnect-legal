Pillars Connect — Terms of Use

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Pillars Connect is a private messaging and calling app for members of Gospel Pillars International ("the church"). By using it you agree to these terms. The app asks you to accept them before you use it, and asks again whenever they change.

Who can use Pillars Connect

  • You must be 18 or older.
  • You must have been invited by the church, and your membership approved by a church administrator.
  • Your account is yours alone. It is tied to your phone number and to one phone at a time. Do not let anyone else use it.

The rules

Pillars Connect is a church community. There is zero tolerance for objectionable content and for abusive members. You must not post, send, share or say in a call anything that:

  • harasses, bullies, threatens or intimidates anyone;
  • is hateful toward anyone because of their race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, sex, disability or any other part of who they are;
  • is sexual, sexually suggestive, or contains nudity;
  • is violent or graphic, or encourages anyone to harm themselves or others;
  • is illegal, or encourages anything illegal;
  • is spam, a scam, or advertising;
  • pretends to be someone else;
  • shares someone else's private information, photos or messages without their permission.

A simple test: if you would not say it in church, do not say it here.

How the rules are enforced

  • Automatic filtering. Messages containing words on the church's list of objectionable words are refused before they are sent.
  • Reporting. You can report a member, a message, a group or someone in a call. Reports go to church administrators, who review them within 24 hours.
  • Blocking. You can block any member. A blocked member cannot message you, call you or find you, and you will not see them.
  • Removal and suspension. Administrators remove content that breaks these rules and suspend or remove the members who posted it. Serious or repeated abuse means losing access for good.
  • The law. Content that may be illegal, or that suggests someone is in danger, may be passed to the authorities.

Your content

What you write, send and share stays yours. You allow the church to store it and deliver it to the people you send it to, which is what the app is for. You are responsible for what you send, and you must have the right to share it.

Only the person who sent a message can delete it, and a deleted message is hidden from everyone rather than erased until the sender's account is deleted. See the Privacy Policy for what is collected, who can see it and how long it is kept.

Calls

Pillars Connect is not a telephone service. It cannot call emergency services. In an emergency, use your phone's own dialler.

Ending your use

You can delete your account at any time in Settings. This removes your profile, your messages and anything you have shared, and cannot be undone. The church may suspend or remove any account that breaks these terms.

No guarantees

The church provides Pillars Connect as it is, to help its members stay in touch. It does its best to keep the app working and your information safe, but it cannot promise the app will always be available or free of faults. As far as the law allows, the church is not responsible for losses that come from using the app or from not being able to use it.
