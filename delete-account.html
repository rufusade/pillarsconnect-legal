Pillars Connect — Deleting your account

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Pillars Connect is the private messaging and calling app of Gospel Pillars International ("the church"), published by ADEWALE OLOSUNDE. This page explains how to delete your Pillars Connect account, and what happens to your information when you do.

In the app

  • Open Pillars Connect and tap your photo or initials at the top right of Chats.
  • Scroll down to Your account and tap Delete my account.
  • Tap Delete everything to confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away. If you are the church's only administrator, the app asks you to make someone else an administrator first.

Without the app

Email sundewale536@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account", and include the phone number your account uses. Before anything is deleted, the church contacts that phone number to make sure the request came from you. Your account is deleted within 30 days of your request, and you will be told when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: your name, the line about yourself, and your photo, including earlier photos you replaced.
  • Your phone number and your ability to sign in.
  • Every message you sent, and everything you shared: photos, videos, voice messages and documents.
  • Your reactions, your place in groups and divisions, the members you blocked, reports you made and reports made about you, your privacy settings, your acceptance of the Terms of Use, and the codes used to send notifications to your phone.

This happens at once and cannot be undone. Copies may remain for a short time in the routine backups our database provider keeps for recovering from faults; they are not used for anything else, and disappear as those backups expire.

What is kept

  • Messages other people sent you stay in their conversations.
  • Announcements you published, and groups and divisions you started, stay for the church, without your name. A photo you set for a group stays with the group.
  • The church's audit log keeps its record of the account — when it joined, was approved and was deleted, and the reason if the church deleted it at your request — under the account's internal number, with no name or phone number.

Deleting some of your data

You can remove some things without deleting your account.

  • A message you sent, with any photo, video, voice message or document in it: press and hold the message, then tap Delete. It disappears for everyone in the conversation straight away. You can delete only messages you sent, not ones sent to you.
  • Your profile photo: tap your photo or initials at the top right of Chats, then tap Remove to take it off your profile, or Change photo to put a different one in its place.
  • The line about yourself: in the same place, clear the About you box and tap Save.
  • Your name: in the same place, clear the Name box and tap Save name. Other members then see your phone number instead.

Once deleted or removed, these are no longer shown to anyone in the app. The text and files of deleted messages, and photos you removed or replaced, stay in our storage until your account is deleted, and are removed with it. To have them removed for good sooner, delete your account.
