Pillars Connect — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Pillars Connect is a private messaging and calling app for members of Gospel Pillars International ("the church"). It is not open to the public: you join by invitation, your membership is approved by a church administrator, and you must be 18 or older. The church is responsible for the information in the app.

What we collect

Your phone number. It is how you sign in and how other members find you. We send a code to it to check the number is yours.

Your name, a line about yourself, and a photo — if you add them. Other members see them.

What you send: messages, photos, videos, voice messages and documents, and who you sent them to.

Whether you are online and when you were last here, and whether you have read a message. You can turn both off in Settings.

A code that identifies your phone — not your phone number, and nothing that names you. Your phone gives it to this app, and it changes if you reset the phone or remove the app. We use it to keep your account signed in on one phone at a time: signing in on a new phone signs the old one out.

Codes for sending you notifications, issued by Apple or Google for your phone.

That you accepted the Terms of Use, and when.

Reports you make, and reports made about you.

One contact at a time, only when you choose it. The app cannot read your address book. If you pick someone with your phone's contact picker, to start a conversation or to invite them, only that one person's number comes to the app, and it is looked up just as if you had typed it. It is not kept, and the name you have saved for them never leaves your phone.

A report when the app crashes or freezes. It says which part of the app went wrong and how, which version of the app it was, the make and model of phone, its system version, language and time zone, and things like how much memory and storage were free. It also lists the last few things the app did before it broke, such as which screen opened or the app going into the background. It does not contain your name, your phone number, your messages or anything else you wrote, your contacts, what was on your screen, or anything that ties it to you or your account; anything of that kind that turns up in an error is removed on your phone before the report is sent, and the internet address it arrives from is not kept. Nothing is sent while the app is working normally. Reports are deleted after 90 days at most.

We do not collect your location, and we use no advertising or tracking of any kind. The only figures about how the app is working that leave your phone are crash reports, used only to find and fix what went wrong, and, if you blur your background on Android, the anonymous figures described under Calls.

Calls

Calls are relayed live by LiveKit and are never recorded.

If you blur your background in a video call, the blurring is done on your phone, before your picture is sent. On Android it uses Google's ML Kit, which sends Google anonymous figures about how well it is working; it never sends your picture.

Who can see your messages

One-to-one messages are visible to you and the person you sent them to. Group messages are visible to the members of that group.

Church administrators cannot read your conversations. They can see who is a member, approve and remove members, and manage groups. When a message is reported, the administrators reviewing the report see that message, with any photo or file in it, and can remove it.

Messages are not end-to-end encrypted. They are encrypted while travelling between your phone and our servers and while stored, and access is restricted by rules enforced on the server, but the service can technically read them. We say this plainly because the difference matters.

Keeping the community safe

Messages, names, the line members write about themselves, group names and announcements are checked against a list of objectionable words before they are saved, and refused if they contain one. This happens on our server and nothing about it is kept.

You can report a member, a message, a group or division, or someone in a call, and block any member. Reports, and what administrators do about them, are kept as a record of how each was handled.

How long we keep things

Messages and media are kept for as long as the church uses Pillars Connect, or until the person who sent them deletes their account. A conversation here is more like a letter kept in a drawer than a word spoken across a room — worth knowing before you write.

Only the person who sent a message can delete it. Deleting a message erases its text and any photo, video, voice message or document in it, for everyone, straight away. A profile photo you remove or replace is deleted too.

Deleting your account (Settings → Your account) removes your profile, your photo, your messages and anything you have shared, straight away. It cannot be undone.

Who processes your information for us

These companies handle information only to provide their part of the app:

  • Supabase — the database, file storage and sign-in. Data is held in the United States.
  • Twilio — sends the sign-in code by text message. It sees your phone number and the code.
  • Apple and Google — deliver notifications to your phone.
  • LiveKit — relays calls while they happen.
  • Google ML Kit — on Android, if you blur your background, receives anonymous figures about how well the blurring works. Never your picture.
  • Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) — receives the crash reports described above. They are held in the European Union.

We do not sell or share your information with anyone else.

Children

Pillars Connect is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly allow anyone younger to use it, and remove accounts that belong to them.

Your choices and rights

In Settings you can change your name, photo and the line about yourself; choose whether members can find you by your number; turn off your online status and read receipts; block members; and delete your account. You can ask the church for a copy of your information, or to correct it, using the contact below.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, the church will tell you in the app, and the date at the top of this page changes.
